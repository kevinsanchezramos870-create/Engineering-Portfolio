Tethered-Sat is the Knights Satellite Club's balloon-based 1U CubeSat platform built to give new members end-to-end mission experience within a semester. T-Sat-0 validated the full mission stack: CubeSat bus, spring-activated Parachute Deployment System (PDS), burn-wire tether release, and onboard sensing.

Spring-activated PDS with open-top canister; multiple FDM iterations validated by drop testing.
Integrated PCB, servo actuator, burn-wire release, and onboard camera into 1U form factor.
